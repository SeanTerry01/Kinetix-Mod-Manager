# WitcherAccess

WitcherAccess is a screen reader accessibility mod for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, made by Pyram for blind and visually impaired players. It reads the game aloud through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS, or the Windows voice), and adds what a blind player needs to explore, fight and follow every quest. It was developed with the Unlocked Spark Foundation (https://unlockedspark.org/en/), which builds games and tools that blind people can use on their own and gives them away free.

This is an offline summary bundled with Kinetix Mod Manager. The mod's authoritative description lives on its Nexus Mods page (https://www.nexusmods.com/witcher3/mods/15538). When you are logged in to Nexus and connected to the internet, reopen this viewer to load the latest description straight from that page. The full player's guide, USER_MANUAL.html, in 18 languages, comes with the mod and is installed in the game folder.

## Features

### What is read aloud

- The whole game is covered: the base game and both expansions, Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine.
- Every menu: the main menu and options, inventory, shops, crafting, alchemy, skills and mutations, the quest journal, the glossary, Gwent, and the game's mod browser.
- Dialogues, choices, subtitles, books, letters and notice boards.
- Audio description of the cutscenes and dialogue scenes, spoken by recorded voices in 17 languages.
- 18 languages in all: the mod speaks the language of your game.

### Finding your way

- A fully accessible world map: its regions, markers and fast travel points, browsed with the keyboard or gamepad.
- An object scanner listing what is around Geralt (people, enemies, containers, doors, quest objectives, clues and places), with distance and direction.
- Navigation assistance that takes you to any target, ladders, climbs, swimming, the horse and the boat included. You can also follow sound beacons placed point by point along the route, or use automatic walking.
- Quest objectives that point where the quest really goes on, and trails followed with Witcher Senses.

### Combat

- Sounds for incoming attacks, dodges, low health and adrenaline.
- Automatic crossbow aiming.

### Help and settings

- The mod's own in-game tutorial, a key glossary (F6), a message history (F3), and F1 help in every menu. These are the keys inside the game.
- Many in-game settings to adapt it to the way you play: every audio cue can be turned on or off, and its volume adjusted. In Kinetix Mod Manager, Ctrl+E on WitcherAccess lists these settings too.
- Keyboard or gamepad (Xbox and PlayStation), with every key remappable. Ctrl+H in Kinetix Mod Manager lists the keys you have.

## Requirements

- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, remastered edition (version 5.0), on Steam or GOG.
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- A screen reader, such as NVDA or JAWS, or the Windows voice.

## Installation

1. Load The Witcher 3 in Kinetix Mod Manager and open its accessibility suite from the Mods menu, Game and Maintenance.
2. Press Install Missing Suite Mods. The manager downloads WitcherAccess from Nexus Mods, asks before running its installer, and then picks the mod up.
3. The installer finds the game, installs the mod, sets its keys, and creates a desktop shortcut: The Witcher 3 with WitcherAccess.
4. Start the game with F5 in Kinetix Mod Manager, or with that shortcut. Both start the same program.

Without Nexus Premium, the manager opens the mod's Nexus page instead and says what to do there.
